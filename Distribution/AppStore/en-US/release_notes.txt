This update supports KyIdentity's latest secure pairing and app sign-in:

- QR pairing proves possession of the pairing token without sending it and verifies the server's reply before saving the pairing.
- App sign-in supports identity tokens bound to the requesting app's proof key.
- Pairing, returning to KyAuth, copied codes and interrupted sign-in requests are more reliable.
- TOTP setup keys of at least 80 bits are supported, with a warning for keys shorter than 128 bits. Existing shorter entries remain available to edit or delete.

For administrators: new QR pairings require a KyIdentity server with pairing proof v1 support. Update KyIdentity before pairing with this version. PIN pairing is unchanged. Existing pairings and encrypted TOTP entries are preserved when updating the app.
